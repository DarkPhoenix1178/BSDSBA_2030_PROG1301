# 🐦 3D Swarming and Vicsek Model
**Assigned Team:** LT 3

## Overview
Develop a three-dimensional simulation of collective motion using the Vicsek Model, with bird flocking as the primary reference.

## Simulation Requirements
- Implement a 3D Vicsek Model.
- Simulate multiple agents moving collectively.
- Allow agents to adjust their movement based on neighboring agents.
- Include adjustable parameters such as interaction radius, speed, and noise.
- Visualize the emergence of collective movement patterns.

## Knowledge-Sharing Presentation
Explain and demonstrate:
- The mathematical foundations of the Vicsek Model.
- Collective behavior and self-organization.
- Local alignment rules and interactions between agents.
- The effects of noise and interaction radius.
- How complex group behavior emerges from simple individual rules.

The group must demonstrate how collective motion arises without centralized control.
