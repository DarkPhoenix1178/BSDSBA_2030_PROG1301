# 🧪 Belousov-Zhabotinsky Reaction
**Assigned Teams:** LT 5 & 6

## Overview
Develop a simulation of the Belousov-Zhabotinsky (BZ) reaction, an oscillating chemical reaction capable of producing repeating color changes and complex wave patterns.

The project will explore how chemical interactions generate self-organizing patterns.

## Simulation Requirements
- Simulate oscillating chemical behavior.
- Visualize changing reaction states through color.
- Demonstrate expanding waves and spiral patterns.
- Implement adjustable parameters influencing reaction behavior.
- Produce a real-time visualization of the reaction.

## Knowledge-Sharing Presentation
Explain and demonstrate:
- What the Belousov-Zhabotinsky reaction is.
- How chemical oscillations occur.
- The principles of reaction-diffusion systems.
- How circular and spiral patterns emerge.
- The mathematical or computational model used to approximate the reaction.

The group must demonstrate an understanding of the chemical and mathematical mechanisms behind the observed patterns.
