# Cohort Extra-Credit Projects

This repository serves as the central workspace for five collaborative simulation projects assigned to the cohort's Learning Teams (LTs).

## Projects

| Project | Assigned Teams |
|---|---|
| Doom Fire | LT 8 & 9 |
| Ripples | LT 1 & 2 |
| Immelmann Turn | LT 4 & 7 |
| Mosh Pits / Swarming / Vicsek Model | LT 3 |
| Belousov-Zhabotinsky Reaction | LT 5 & 6 |

## Project Requirements

Each group is expected to:

1. Develop a functional simulation of its assigned phenomenon.
2. Demonstrate a strong understanding of the mathematical, scientific, and computational concepts involved.
3. Document the implementation and underlying principles.
4. Prepare a knowledge-sharing presentation explaining the concepts, implementation, and results.
5. Demonstrate the completed simulation during the presentation.

## Knowledge-Sharing Presentations

Presentations will be scheduled upon completion of each project.

Groups must demonstrate **adept understanding** of their assigned topic, including the theoretical foundations, implementation methods, and behavior of their simulation.

The objective is not merely to produce a working simulation but to understand and communicate the principles behind it.

## Extra-Credit Progression

**Completion of all five collaborative projects is required to unlock personal projects for extra credit.**

Personal projects constitute the next stage of the cohort's extra-credit activities.
