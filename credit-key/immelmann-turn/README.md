# ✈️ Immelmann Turn and Aerial Maneuvers
**Assigned Teams:** LT 4 & 7

## Overview
Develop a 3D simulation of the Immelmann Turn, an aerobatic maneuver that reverses an aircraft's heading by approximately 180 degrees while increasing its altitude.

The simulation must also accommodate several additional aerial maneuvers.

## Simulation Requirements
- Simulate an Immelmann Turn.
- Implement additional aerial maneuvers, such as loops, barrel rolls, and Split-S turns.
- Visualize aircraft position and orientation in 3D.
- Provide controls for selecting and observing different maneuvers.

## Knowledge-Sharing Presentation
Explain and demonstrate:
- The principles behind each aerial maneuver.
- Three-dimensional coordinate systems and transformations.
- Aircraft orientation, rotation, and movement.
- The mathematical methods used to simulate flight paths.
- How different maneuvers affect the aircraft's position, heading, and altitude.

The group must demonstrate a clear understanding of the mathematical and physical principles governing its simulations.
